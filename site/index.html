How the six transaction groups are defined

Every mainnet transaction in the pinned block range is replayed under the candidate gas schedule and categorised into exactly one of these six groups. The three changed groups — Succeeds with changes, Fixable, and Potentially broken — require a working baseline (the transaction succeeded before the repricing); a transaction whose baseline replay already failed is Already failing, so it can never be counted as newly broken.

No change g1

Identical outcome and execution trace under the repricing — the transaction is completely unaffected.

Succeeds with changes g2

Still succeeds at its original gas limit, but something changed: gas used, event logs, or output/trace. Needs a working baseline.

Fixable with gas-limit increase g3

Worked before; now runs out of gas at the original limit, but completes once the limit is raised (rescued at up to 10×). A gas-limit bump fixes it.

Potentially broken g4

Worked before; now fails and is not rescued even at the 10× gas ceiling — genuinely at risk under the repricing.

Already failing af

The baseline replay itself failed, so a schedule-side failure is not newly broken. Includes permanent fail→fail rows and baseline-fail→success rescues.

Unknown g5

No retained per-transaction detail — dropped at the producer’s per-block drill-in cap. A coverage gap, not an outcome; zero on non-truncated blocks.
