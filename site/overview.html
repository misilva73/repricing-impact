Fixable with gas-limit increase — breakdown

Transactions that fail at the original gas limit but complete in replay with a higher limit of up to 10×. Completion in replay does not establish that the required limit is practical under block or protocol constraints.

Gas-limit increase required

Measured minimum gas needed as a multiple of the original transaction limit. Bins are upper-inclusive; for example, “2–4×” means more than 2× and at most 4×.
