Affected contracts

Search a single contract to see what happens to it under the repricing. A contract is affected if it appears in any Potentially-broken (G4) transaction — as the entry contract the user calls, the out-of-gas halt site, or the non-OOG revert site. Results are organized as a ranked list of distinct failure modes (clusters), each annotated with the repricing drivers behind it. Freshly-deployed accounts that run out of gas during their own creation (mostly ERC-4337 smart-account wallets) are collapsed into a shared summary view rather than an individual page. If the analysis cannot be loaded, the page reports that separately rather than treating the contract as unaffected.
